Freight Operations & Vehicle Fleet

We operate an extensive fleet including 20ft/24ft single-axle closed containers, 32ft single & multi-axle (MXL/SXL) vehicles, 40ft high-cube trailers, and specialized low-bed/semi-low-bed trailers for heavy engineering machinery up to 35 MT standard (and 120 MT for specialized ODC).
Our high-frequency corridors connect Coimbatore with Chennai, Tuticorin (V.O.C. Port), Cochin, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai/JNPT, Pune, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, and Delhi-NCR with round-the-clock driver crew rotations.

Telematics, E-Waybill & Compliance

Every dispatched vehicle is tracked through our TILS SecureMesh™ IoT telematics. Clients receive a dedicated web portal link or ERP webhook feed delivering 30-second GPS location refresh intervals, automated geofence ingress/egress timestamps, and digital seal alerts.
Our central Coimbatore dispatch desk operates a 24/7 compliance monitoring unit. If a vehicle encounters unpreventable highway bottlenecks or severe weather, our system automatically initiates E-Waybill validity extensions within statutory GST timelines to guarantee zero checkpoint penalties.
Immediately upon dock unloading and consignee sign-off, our drivers capture a cryptographically verified, timestamped e-POD via mobile terminals, which is instantly accessible in the client billing dashboard within minutes.
